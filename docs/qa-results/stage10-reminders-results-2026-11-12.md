## Requirements and implementation inspected

- `README.md` identifies invoice reminders as local WorkManager work. `docs/PHASE3_UPDATE.md` requires reconciliation after restore and retention of the delivery ledger; `docs/PHASE3_VERIFICATION.md` records that prior delivery verification was incomplete.
- `InvoiceReminderPolicy.kt` defines Cambodia time, 09:00 delivery, due-soon at three days before due, due-today, then first overdue one day after due and weekly thereafter. It does not replay a missed pre-due reminder late.
- The production worker is `com.example.roomkit.Date.Workers.InvoiceReminderWorker`. `InvoiceReminderScheduler` enqueues a six-hour unique sweep, a reconciliation request, and per-invoice timed work. The app uses AndroidX’s default WorkManager initialization; no custom worker factory was found.
- The worker loads the invoice and active payments, checks eligibility, atomically claims the `(invoice, due date, slot)` in the delivery ledger, posts the notification, then queues the next slot. The claim happens before posting, so a process interruption between those operations can leave a claimed delivery without a visible notification. Notifications use tag `invoice_{id}` and open the invoice detail with that invoice ID.
- Eligible invoices have a positive remaining balance and an issued/unpaid/partial/overdue/paid status. Drafts and voided invoices are excluded. A paid status with remaining balance can remain eligible under the current policy.

- **Cause confirmed in production source and read-only scheduling evidence.** `InvoiceReminderScheduler.enqueueNext()` builds a unique work name from invoice ID, due-date timestamp, and policy slot key, then enqueues with `ExistingWorkPolicy.KEEP`. Before and after the emulator clock jump, invoice 12 still mapped to the same `DUE_SOON` slot for Dec 5, so the name remained `invoice_reminder_v2_12_1796489999999_DUE_SOON`. The saved JobScheduler state showed the delay still about 4 days after normal app reconciliation. This is intentional `KEEP` behavior at the unique-work policy level; it is not a failed recomputation of the slot.
- **Reconciliation behavior.** `MainActivity.onPostResume()` calls `InvoiceReminderScheduler.start()`. `start()` enqueues generic reconciliation with `KEEP`; the worker reads current invoice/payment state, recalculates the policy slot, and tries to enqueue the invoice-specific request. Because the key and slot are unchanged, the existing delayed request is retained. No RoomKit receiver or other app handler for `TIME_SET`, `DATE_CHANGED`, or `TIMEZONE_CHANGED` was found, so a wall-clock/time-zone change does not itself trigger a rebase. The observed generic reconciliation worker succeeded, but did not shorten invoice 12’s pending JobScheduler delay.

The saved JobScheduler snapshot after the clock jump put its next execution roughly 52 minutes away, before the Dec 2 09:00 `DUE_SOON` slot; a subsequent sweep could execute inside the slot’s same-day window and deliver if eligible and unclaimed. This is a code-based fallback, not a timely-delivery guarantee: periodic execution is inexact and Android may defer it. No invoice-12 delivery was observed from this fallback.
