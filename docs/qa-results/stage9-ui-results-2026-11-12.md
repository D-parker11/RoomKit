## Date, logs, and limitations

Historical dates were accepted through the UI date picker. The meter screen uses the emulator’s current date for the saved event timestamp, so the test emulator date was changed in event order using Android Settings only: Nov 9 for A’s monthly reading, Nov 10 for A’s move-out/final reading, and Nov 11 for B’s move-in, reading, and payment. Original automatic date/time and time-zone were enabled at start. After verification, Android Settings was used to restore automatic date/time. Final settings: `auto_time=1`, `auto_time_zone=1`; emulator date returned to Nov 12, 2026 (+07). The Mac clock was not changed.

RoomKit Logcat was checked. The latest RoomKit fatal entries in retained logs were from Nov 10 and show the previously fixed `RoomsFragment` RecyclerView LayoutManager initialization crash. No RoomKit fatal exception occurred during this Nov 11 test run. Old log entries were not cleared.

The QA-901 room card continues to display the room-level default rent of 140,000 KHR while showing active tenant Nine B. The move-in confirmation and tenancy record show B’s actual 200,000 KHR tenancy rent, and B’s invoice correctly uses 200,000. This is a room-card display distinction to retain for follow-up, not a discrepancy in the saved tenancy or invoice. The separate check that B assignment is prevented while A remains active, and opening Nine A’s tenant profile after restart, were not executed and remain explicitly NOT TESTED.

No app data was cleared, no database was edited, and no production code was changed. No real payment, commit, or push occurred. Existing unrelated local source and test changes were preserved.
